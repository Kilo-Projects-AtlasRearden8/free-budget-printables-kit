# Free Budget Printables Kit

Fillable, printable, large-print friendly budget printables from Asdale Dynamics, LLC. No account needed. Nothing is saved or sent anywhere — it runs entirely on your device.

## What's inside

| Sheet | What it does |
|-------|--------------|
| [Monthly Budget Planner](monthly-budget-planner.html) | Plan income vs. expenses by category, with planned vs. actual totals and variance. |
| [Expense Tracker](expense-tracker.html) | Log every purchase by date, category, and amount with running monthly totals. |
| [Bill Calendar](bill-calendar.html) | Mark bill due dates on a monthly calendar and track what's been paid. |
| [Savings Goal Sheet](savings-goal-sheet.html) | Set a target, track contributions, and watch your progress toward the goal. |

## How to use

1. Open any sheet in your browser (Chrome, Edge, Safari, or Firefox).
2. Fill it in on screen, or print it blank and fill it in by hand.
3. Press `Ctrl+P` (`Cmd+P` on Mac) to print. Buttons and controls disappear on paper.
4. No sign-up, no account, and nothing is sent anywhere — it runs entirely on your device.

## Designed for accessibility

- Large-print friendly (18px base on screen, 16px on paper)
- High-contrast black-on-white output
- Big touch targets and clear labels
- Works printed or on screen
